This product is a rebuild of the PyCon US mobile app
(https://github.com/PyCon/pycon-us-mobile), which is licensed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0. The original app is our functional reference;
its data semantics, texts and research notes informed this project.

The app bundles the Roboto font (Regular, Medium and Bold, version 2.138,
https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto), Copyright 2011 Google Inc., licensed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0. Its files and license are in
flutter_packages/roboto_fonts/.

The brand marks in src/assets/icons/brands/ (Python, PyPI, Mastodon, Bluesky,
X, LinkedIn, YouTube and Discord) are the unmodified SVGs of Simple Icons,
version 13.21.0 (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), dedicated to
the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. The marks themselves are trademarks
of their owners; the app shows each one only beside a link to that brand.

The floor plans in src/assets/maps/ are the PyCon US mobile app's
(src/assets/img/floor-plans/, Apache License, Version 2.0), drawings of the
Long Beach Convention Center's spaces prepared by GES, re-encoded to WebP at a
smaller size; on the campus plan, the PyCon US wordmark and the venue's logo
are painted over. The expo hall plan is that app's too
(src/assets/img/pycon-us-2026-floorplan-nologo.jpg), re-encoded the same way,
with its sun-and-palms mark painted over; the sponsors' logos on it are
trademarks of their owners. The room and venue pin positions and the booth
rectangles in src/pycon_us/data/2026/map.toml are that app's too.
